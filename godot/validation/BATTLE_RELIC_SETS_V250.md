# Personal relic-set combat validation — 2.5.0

The battle model receives a deep copy of `ownerEffects` on each ally. Permanent set statistics arrive through the existing computed-stat pipeline. Battle construction consumes those statistics once and keeps personal effects separate from the team-wide rogue effect pool.

## Effect semantics

Flame's conditional direct-damage contribution enters the existing status-damage bucket. Wind compares current effective caster and target speed, with equality producing the ordinary hit. Its conditional multiplier is a separate bucket. Personal conditional bonuses also apply to direct follow-up hits; the established follow-up return boundary preserves trigger isolation.

Aurora's two thresholds add to a 30% active-skill healing modifier. That personal modifier multiplies the established rogue healing modifier. A living target receiving an active heal qualifies for the five-energy action trigger, including a full-HP target. Regeneration, lifesteal and passive recovery keep their original invocation paths and coefficients. Shana's existing passive conversion continues to derive its amount from the active heal it observes.

Thunder grants four energy once per action containing a direct critical hit. Multiple targets, repeated damage effects and fully absorbed critical hits share that action trigger. Subsequent accepted actions reset the trigger. Existing character energy passives retain their per-hit rules. All energy additions retain the physical 100-energy bound.

Stone enters the existing direct-damage reduction bucket for its wearer. Rogue reductions, status reductions and Lorin's block converge on the existing final 90% reduction ceiling. Shields then absorb the remainder completely when sufficient capacity exists. Poison, burn and invincibility keep their established paths.

Star multiplies the shield amount generated by a skill's shield effect by 1.25. A legacy 20% shield cast onto a recipient with 2,000 maximum HP therefore gives 500 shield. The caster supplies the set effect; the recipient supplies the legacy HP source. Equipment condition capacity, opening blessing shields, healing-trigger utility shields and passive overflow conversion keep their existing amounts.

## Shared skill-scaling contract

An individual skill effect may declare `scalingStat` as `atk`, `maxHp` or `def`. Damage and healing retain ATK as their default source. A shield effect with an omitted declaration retains its recipient-maximum-HP source and `value` coefficient. An explicit shield declaration selects the caster's stated source and uses `multiplier`, falling back to its existing `value`. Effective ATK and DEF include the corresponding combat modifiers; maximum HP uses the caster's maximum rather than current HP.

`effect_source_value(caster, effect, target, raw)` provides the common source. `skill_effect_amount(caster, effect, target)` supplies active healing and skill shield amounts. `predict_effect_damage(caster, target, skill, effect, critical, follow_up, chain, blocked)` provides the integer direct hit before shield absorption. `expected_effect_damage` weights the exact critical and Lorin-block outcomes. These queries preserve state and combat RNG. `action_energy_gain`, `effective_speed` and `owner_effect_sum` support the loadout reference evaluator through the same rules.
