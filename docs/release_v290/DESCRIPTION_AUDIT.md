# Thunder description audit

2026-11-10. Static comparison of `godot/data/catalog.json` and `godot/data/thunder_combat_patch.json` against `battle_model.gd`, `game_model.gd` and `relic_sets.gd`. Combat parameters and approved story text remain unchanged. Runtime checks are coordinated in the release validation window.

## Coverage

All 12 skills were compared for source, target, coefficient, status, duration and armor unlocks. Raisa normal/skill are 120% single and 90% all; armor conversion uses half current HP (rounded down), equal shield and four rounds including entry. Armor normal is 170% plus 100% piercing; skill is 245%/120% on primary and 160%/85% on up to two others; ultimate is 270%/140% then 180%/95%. Piercing uses `rawAtk`, ignores defense and has zero crit chance. The chain floor keeps both secondary targets at the stated coefficient. Unlock stages and locked 100 energy match the descriptions.

Vika's three skills apply 35% defense reduction before 210% single, 130% all and 320% all damage, for two/three/four rounds. Eve's normal is 150% single plus 25% healing to the lowest HP-ratio ally; skill is 200% attack shield for three rounds plus 45% healing; ultimate is 300% shield for four rounds, 90% healing and three-round regeneration. Regeneration uses the existing 8% recipient maximum HP per round rule. Same-source skill shields retain the greater shield and refresh duration; regeneration retains the greater duration.

All three passives and all 18 resolved constellation entries were checked. Generic C1/C3/C5 damage bonuses, C2 positive-cooldown reduction and C4 HP/defense bonuses flow through computed stats; Raisa C5 replaces the generic ultimate bonus with 30% action energy gain. Raisa C6 extends armor to five rounds, allowing the stated second ultimate in an uninterrupted rotation. Vika C6 multiplies the full follow-up coefficient after relic/blessing additions by 1.15. Eve's living shield aura is 10%/14% and her end-round shielded healing is 22%/28% effective attack with two-round regeneration.

All six bonuses across storm_armor, storm_tracer and storm_shelter were checked. Two- and four-piece stat bonuses accumulate. Armor amplification applies to native skill damage; tracer adds 8 percentage points to its wearer's conduct coefficient and refreshes a two-round party armor buff when the wearer applies conduct through a skill. Shelter gives 15% + 15% active shield amount, 20% active healing and five energy once per healing action, including a healing normal attack and full-HP healing attempts. Passive healing, regeneration and armor HP conversion use their separate paths.

All 12 unique blessings were checked: thunder_shelter_white, thunder_mark_blue, thunder_link_gold; raisa_charge_white, raisa_armor_blue, raisa_armor_gold; vika_mark_white, vika_link_blue, vika_open_gold; eve_shield_white, eve_guard_blue, eve_dawn_gold. Each character has three shared and three personal entries, totaling six. Shared IDs occur once in the pool and receive one shared draw weight. Repeated earned copies retain existing additive effect behavior. Values match: 12% shielded direct damage, 18% marked direct damage, 24% linked piercing; 12% charge, 15%/20% armor damage; +1 mark round, +4/+8 percentage-point conduct coefficients and opening three-round 35% mark; 15% Eve active shields, 15% regeneration-conditioned damage reduction subject to the common 90% ceiling, and opening 15% recipient-HP shields for three rounds plus two-round regeneration. Opening effects apply at battle start; later waves follow the existing wave rules.

## Corrections

Ten description fields were corrected identically in catalog and patch. Raisa's passive, C5 and charge blessing now specify normal/skill action energy rather than implying every energy source. The two shared damage blessings and both armor-related relic bonuses now specify native normal/skill/ultimate direct damage, reflecting the separate follow-up multiplier. Vika C6 now identifies 20.7% as the baseline example and explicitly includes relic/blessing coefficient additions. Vika's passive and linked blessing now specify damage reaching HP and a surviving target; the follow-up hit tracker is updated after shield absorption and the action hook selects living enemies. Their attack source is identified as panel attack.

## Verification and remaining scope

Python parsed both JSON files, verified exactly the intended ten string changes per file, compared every thunder character/skill/C6/relic entry and every blessing entry between patch and catalog, and confirmed twelve unique IDs and six eligible blessings per character. All checks passed. The string-only transformation preserved every other field, including story prose. Existing generic descriptions and numerical mechanics were retained. This audit establishes source-level consistency; actual UI rendering, control-interrupted armor rotations, shielded-hit edge cases and combat regression execution belong to the final runtime evidence rather than this static audit.
